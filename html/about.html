About Us

The story behind Touche

Our Story

Touche was founded in 2000 with love and passion, with the goal of making every guest feel welcome while enjoying traditional food and drinks. Beyond its delicious dishes and freshly brewed coffee, Touche is known for its warm and inviting atmosphere. Over the years, it has become one of the city's favorite dining spots, bringing people together through great food and unforgettable experiences. And our story doesn't end here! Touche is excited to announce its plans to expand to Germany, bringing its unique flavors and hospitality to a new destination.
